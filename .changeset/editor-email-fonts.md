Add email-safe font families to the theme: `EMAIL_FONTS` (web-safe stacks plus Inter, Roboto, Open Sans and Lato), a `fontFamily` theme property and a "Font" input in the basic theme's text panel. The body font is repeated on the container table for Outlook, and web fonts used by the theme or a `fontFamily` mark are linked in the email `<head>`.
